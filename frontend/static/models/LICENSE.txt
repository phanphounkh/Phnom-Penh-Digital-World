CampusEats Original 3D Assets

All GLB models in this directory are original assets created by the accompanying
Blender scripts in tools/blender/ (generate_assets.py for the CampusEats models;
generate_avatars.py, generate_city_assets.py, generate_shop_assets.py and
generate_vehicle_assets.py for Phnom Penh Digital World). No third-party models are included.
