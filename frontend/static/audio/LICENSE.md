# Action sound assets

These WAV files are original procedural project audio, released under CC0 1.0.
They are generated by `tools/audio/generate_action_audio.py` using Python's standard library.
No third-party recordings, paid services or microphone recordings are used.
They are synthesized effects, not recordings of specific Cambodian vehicles or locations.

## Background music — Riverside Breeze

`riverside-breeze.mp3` is an original synthesized instrumental composition for this
project, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
No samples or third-party recordings are used. It is decorative game music, not a
recording of traditional Cambodian music. Duration: 51.2 seconds, looped; stereo
32 kHz MP3 at 96 kbps, approximately 616 KB. Reproduce with
`python3 tools/audio/generate_music.py` (NumPy and FFmpeg required for generation;
no additional browser dependency).
