# Phnom Penh photograph reference catalog

Priority study: Wat Phnom and Daun Penh. These are real photographs, not generated images.
Camera coordinates locate the photographer; they are not building placement anchors.
Image pixels were reviewed outside Git and are not used in runtime textures.

Date/time values come from the source and have not had their timezone independently verified.
The 2012–2022 photos document historic appearance, not a current survey. The JSON catalog
also records the retrieval date, original URLs, observed features, provenance and preview hashes.

Wat Phnom stair, entrance and clock-garden photos were compared together. They informed
the grass/soil material study and identify future landmark work. The Preah Ang Yukanthor
photo demonstrates projecting floor slabs, frames, shutters and awnings; it is a style reference,
not an identification of the particular buildings receiving procedural trim in the game.
The Central Market aerial is catalogued for a later landmark asset pass.

Original OSM/PostGIS positions, footprints and recorded/estimated height fields remain the
placement source. The current terrain mound and generated building appearances are still
approximations. No camera coordinates have been substituted for landmark coordinates.

Royal Palace, Sisowath Quay, National Museum and Independence Monument remain pending
photo-study/model milestones; their catalog entries are not claimed complete.
