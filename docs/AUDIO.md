# Game audio and playback recovery

## Current action playback

Walking/running/sprinting footsteps, landing, motorbike and bicycle sounds now use
**standard HTMLAudioElement playback of local WAV files**, independently of Web Audio.
`frontend/src/lib/game/audio/FileAudio.ts` owns bounded step voices and looping vehicle players;
`WorldAudio.ts` coordinates saved settings and optional Web Audio ambience.

- Footsteps follow actual travelled distance; run/sprint use a heavier clip and faster cadence.
- Motorbike idle/throttle changes volume and playback rate; bicycles never play the motor clip.
- Bicycles play separate pedal/chain and coasting/freewheel loops while actually rolling.
- Paused movement and stationary bicycle wheels produce no rolling effects. Vehicle loops fade
  to zero on dismount. Background tabs pause media; world teardown releases the players.
- **Settings → Sound → Effects volume** controls action sounds. Master, ambience and mute
  are separate and saved in browser local storage (`ppw-audio-v1`).

The right-toolbar speaker and **Enable / test sound** explicitly unmute the game, restore
zero Master/Effects levels to audible defaults, and play the test WAV directly inside the
click handler. They do not await an AudioContext first. Normal nonzero preferences and OS
volume are preserved. Settings show errors and status.

## Direct playback diagnostic

Open **http://localhost:3000/audio/test-tone.wav** and press Play in Chrome's audio player.
This bypasses the entire game/audio graph and tests the same-origin file and browser output.
It is distinct from an internal analyser reporting a nonzero signal. Physical audible
confirmation from the user's live tab/speakers remains separate from automated tests.

## Sources and resource use

The seven PCM WAV clips are original synthesized project effects generated by
`tools/audio/generate_action_audio.py` with Python's standard library. They are CC0, mono,
24 kHz / 16 bit, and total under 400 KB. There are no downloaded recordings, paid services,
microphone permissions, or claims about recording a particular Cambodian vehicle/location.

Native media is loaded lazily after a gesture. Step voices are pooled; three vehicle loops
are reused. AudioContext is optional for wind, nearby mapped-water/road ambience and daytime
chirps; unavailable Web Audio does not suppress action sounds. Road/surface cues and bird
calls remain decorative approximations, not surveyed acoustic data.

## Verification

- Frontend check, 230 unit tests and both legacy test files passed; five targeted audio tests
  passed after the replacement. `tools/audio/test_audio.py` confirms all seven WAVs decode,
  contain nonzero samples and remain within PCM headroom.
- `tests/perf/media-audio.mjs` disables AudioContext in Chrome, then verifies the direct WAV
  response, advancing test playback and a decoded/playing footstep voice at nonzero volume.
- `tests/perf/vehicle-audio.mjs` exercises garage, navigation, mounting and driving; it checks
  actual HTML media loop playback/time/volume as well as movement state.
- Earlier RMS-based reports describe the former Web Audio action path. They do **not** prove
  that the user's speakers were audible and are not validation of the new media output.

The no-AudioContext browser test and actual file-based bicycle/motorbike gameplay tests
passed on the deployed frontend. Reports: [media playback](perf/media-audio-smoke.json),
[vehicle media](perf/file-vehicle-audio-smoke.json). These establish loading/decoding,
advancing media playback and nonzero element volume, not a physical listening test.

No backend, avatar model, geographic data or GitHub state is changed by the audio rollout.

## Background music

“Riverside Breeze” is a quiet original instrumental, looped from a local 616 KB MP3.
It starts after a player gesture. **Settings → Sound → Music volume** defaults to
25%; Master and Mute also apply. Effects and ambience controls remain independent.
Saved preferences without a music field migrate to the quiet default.
`BackgroundMusic.ts` owns a single native media element, pauses in background tabs,
preserves playback position when muted, and releases media on world teardown.
No AudioContext is required for music. The track is original CC0 synthesis; source
and terms are in `tools/audio/generate_music.py` and `static/audio/LICENSE.md`.
Direct listening: http://localhost:3000/audio/riverside-breeze.mp3 .

Music validation: frontend check reported zero errors/warnings; 245 unit tests and
three Node asset tests passed. `tests/perf/music.mjs` passed against the rebuilt
local frontend in Chrome with AudioContext disabled: no playback before interaction,
advancing decoded music, separate volume, mute/unmute, crossing the loop boundary,
saved music preferences after reload, and simultaneous walking effects. No page
errors were reported. Raw report: `docs/perf/music-smoke.json`. Actual settings
screenshot: `/tmp/ppg-background-music-settings.png`. Automated media playback does
not establish physical speaker audibility. GPU/FPS was not benchmarked for this change;
one reused media element adds no rendering geometry or per-frame music processing.
